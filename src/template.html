The plant geometry and normalized operating examples illustrate an integration concept. Site duty and final equipment selection depend on LNG composition, pressure, flow, exchanger pinch, auxiliaries and send-out constraints.

Storage reference
~111 K for LNG near storage pressure. The pumped-stream inlet can differ.
Cold-end approach
For a direct exchanger, TH₂,out ≥ TLNG,in + ΔTmin. A tighter pinch can occur inside the exchanger.
3D assembly views
The exchanger stack and cold-box internals show representative functions and flow directions. Plate spacing in the exploded view is exaggerated.
Final liquefaction
~20 K for liquid hydrogen near atmospheric pressure. Mechanical deep refrigeration and staged ortho–para conversion remain part of the train.
Dispatch example
Recovered duty is the lesser of available usable LNG cold and the precooling demand. Trim duty covers the remainder. These controls use percentages of an illustrative duty basis.
