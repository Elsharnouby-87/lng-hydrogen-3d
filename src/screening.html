Calculation basis and sources

Precooling uses 2,471.48 kJ/kg LH₂ from interpolated ideal-gas enthalpy. Total heat removal is approximated as 5,100 kJ/kg LH₂. Available duty is LNG flow in t/h × usable uptake in kJ/kg ÷ 3,600. Heat applied to the reference plant is capped at 1.430 MWth.

Full-duty electrical benefit uses 0.362 kWh/kg reversible work divided by assumed second-law efficiency, less incremental auxiliaries. The calculator scales this net benefit with the fraction of reference precooling duty met.
