# Industrial scene materials

The following CC0 assets are reduced for the self-contained real-time viewer.

- Poly Haven, [Rough Concrete](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_concrete). Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps.
- Poly Haven, [Clean Asphalt](https://polyhaven.com/a/clean_asphalt). Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps.
- Poly Haven, [Overcast Industrial Courtyard](https://polyhaven.com/a/overcast_industrial_courtyard). Equirectangular HDR lighting.
- ambientCG, [Metal 051 A](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal051A). OpenGL normal and roughness maps.

The marine terminal and equipment geometry are original conceptual illustrations. Photographs of Al-Zour and Dunkerque, the ALPEMA construction sketch and Sumitomo cold-box photographs informed the model.
